Amazon Prime Video · a tribute to Jackie Shroff

Launched on April Fool’s Day, the campaign imagined a fictional “Jaggu Dada Mode” of Shroff’s style. It crossed 48 million views and 53,000 shares to become Prime Video’s top asset in two years. ABOUT ME

I’m an architect who fell for storytelling. Today I work as a Production Designer and Interior Stylist — building sets for television commercials and films that make the characters of a story belong to their space, and designing homes with that same instinct for narrative.

Over 10+ years I’ve designed for Amazon Prime Video, Netflix, Flipkart, Croma, Hershey’s, Amanté, Taneira and many more — collaborating with directors and production teams on everything from intimate photoshoots to large commercial shoots. My work spans concept development, technical drawing and set construction, ensuring every design enhances the story while meeting real deadlines and budgets. That range has sharpened my adaptability, problem-solving and attention to detail under pressure.

Alongside film, I design and style residential interiors — 1, 3 and 4 BHK homes across Mumbai, many in collaboration with the studio Attirail — layering colour, pattern, art and collected objects into rooms that feel genuinely lived-in.

— Shivani

How I work
the approach

STORY FIRST, ALWAYS

Whether it’s a 30-second film or a family home, the process is the same: understand the story, design the world, and sweat every detail until it feels inevitable.

01

Understand

Script, brand, brief, or the way a family actually lives — I start by finding the narrative and the feeling to design toward.

02

Design

Concept development, references, technical drawings and material palettes — turning intent into a buildable, budgeted plan.

03

Realise

Set construction, dressing and styling on-set or on-site — problem-solving under pressure so the final frame feels effortless.
